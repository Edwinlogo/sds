Legal & Privacy

Safe Drive Services Privacy Policy

Last Updated November 2026
Website safedriveservice.com
Headquarters Orlando, Florida, United States.

At Safe Drive Services (SDS), we respect your right to privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect the personal information you provide when interacting with our websites, online forms, social media channels, or messaging applications (WhatsApp, SMS).

1. Information We Collect

When you complete a recruitment form or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Identification and contact details: Full name, phone number, email address, city, and state or ZIP code.
  • Employment eligibility information: Driver's license status, driving record (MVR), U.S. work authorization status, certifications (such as CPR/First Aid), and screening results (Background Check and Drug Test).
  • Vehicle information: Make, model, year, registration status, auto insurance, and mechanical inspection (when using a personal vehicle applies).

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data solely to:

  • Evaluate and process applications from drivers interested in providing services with SDS.
  • Verify compliance with safety standards required by school districts and state laws.
  • Contact you by phone call, email, SMS, or WhatsApp to advance your onboarding process or inform you about available routes.
  • Coordinate logistics and operational assignment of specialized student transportation services.

3. Disclosure and Transfer of Data to Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Your data may only be shared under the following conditions:

  • Verification providers: Accredited companies responsible for conducting criminal background reports, substance screenings, and driving record checks, always under strict confidentiality.
  • Legal compliance and school districts: Regulatory authorities or contracting entities when strictly required by law or school regulations for service delivery.

4. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or improper disclosure.

5. Communications and Consent

By providing your data through forms or direct messages, you authorize Safe Drive Services to contact you regarding service opportunities. You may revoke this consent or opt out of our communications at any time by replying "STOP" to a message or requesting it in writing.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored in our databases, except for records we are required to retain under legal or contractual regulations in the transportation sector.
